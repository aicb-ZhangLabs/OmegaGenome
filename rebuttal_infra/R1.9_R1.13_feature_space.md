## Results (PI metrics)

### FULL-SAMPLE results (n=3000, the whole splice_sites_all test set) — reproduces the paper

> **Root cause of the prior n=300 mismatch (cosine 0.064 vs paper 0.411): it was NOT sample size,
> it was the TEACHER feature definition.** The paper figure (`Combined_KD_splice_sites_all_distinct5_hist_260204`)
> comes from `umap-tsne-260114_panel_v13_260204.py`, which feeds the **`second_to_last`** feature to
> the t-SNE/cosine. For Enformer that is `embeddings[:, -L//4:, :].mean(dim=1)` — the mean over the
> **last quarter of trunk sequence positions** — NOT the full-sequence pooled mean. The prior
> `extract_feats_logits.py` used the full pooled mean (`return_features=True`), which deflated the
> cosine ~7×. After switching the teacher extraction to the last-quarter `second_to_last` (BPNet
> students' `second_to_last` already equals their pooled feature, so they were unchanged), the
> full-sample numbers land on the paper. The cosine *computation* (StandardScaler → per-model PCA to
> common_dim=50 → `1-cosine` per sample) was already identical to the PI script.

t-SNE **silhouette** (Row-1) and **logit Pearson r** (class 0, Row-2) — full sample n=3000:

| panel | silhouette (ours) | paper | logit r class 0 (ours) | paper |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| Enformer teacher | **0.418** | 0.424 | — | — |
| OmegaGenome student | **0.398** | 0.415 | **0.777** | 0.779 |
| DKD student | 0.216 | — | 0.698 | — |
| BPNet from-scratch | 0.198 | 0.227 | 0.674 | 0.677 |

Sample-wise **cosine similarity** (panel F/D, PCA-aligned 50-d, `1-cosine`) — full sample n=3000:

| comparison | cosine mean ± std | median | paper mean | t-test p (TS vs TB) |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| teacher vs **OmegaGenome** | **0.399 ± 0.295** | 0.470 | **0.411** | 2.2e-193 |
| teacher vs **from-scratch baseline** | **0.157 ± 0.312** | 0.165 | **0.163** | — |
| teacher vs **DKD** | −0.003 ± 0.297 | −0.004 | — | 1.6e-89 |

**Read (R1.9):** yes — a substantial part of OmegaGenome's edge is feature preservation. At full
sample with the paper's `second_to_last` feature, OmegaGenome's student recovers the teacher's class
organization (silhouette 0.398 ≈ teacher 0.418), tracks the teacher's logits best (r=0.777), and has
by far the highest mean teacher-cosine (**0.399**, vs from-scratch BPNet **0.157**; two-sided Welch
t-test p=2.2e-193). This reproduces the paper's headline cosine 0.411/0.163 and silhouette
0.424/0.415/0.227. DKD's student (silhouette 0.216, cosine −0.003) and the scratch baseline are
markedly worse-aligned.

## R1.13 — the OTHER teachers (NT-2.5B / DNABERT-2 / Caduceus), full sample n=3000

The paper figure only showed Enformer. R1.13 asks for the other teachers. Same 2×3 panel per teacher
(Teacher | its distilled OmegaGenome student | the SAME teacher-agnostic from-scratch BPNet baseline),

### BOTH-LAYER matrix (2026-06-30): all 5 teachers, FEATURE-ALIGNED (mse>0) students, [-2] paper-method + [-1] MSE-aligned

**Carbon [-1] is the cleanest demonstration:** at [-2] the 3072-dim carbon teacher gives the PCA-cosine
artifact (student −0.077 < baseline +0.149), but at the **MSE-aligned [-1] layer the cosine flips and
strongly orders** (student **+0.101 > baseline −0.177**, p=3e-134) — the carbon student WAS trained with
mse=1 feature alignment, and the layer it was aligned to is exactly where its features sit closest to the
teacher. Carbon also has the highest student silhouette (0.471) and logit-r (0.825) of any teacher.

**Read:** at the **[-2] paper-method feature**, the feature-aligned OmegaGenome student's teacher-cosine
BEATS the from-scratch baseline for every teacher (Enformer +0.399>+0.157, NT +0.048>−0.211, DNABERT-2
+0.078>+0.007, Caduceus −0.002>−0.295; all p≪1e-7). Silhouette (S≈0.40-0.47 ≫ B 0.20) and class-0 logit-r
(S 0.73-0.82 ≫ B 0.61-0.70) favor the student at BOTH layers for ALL teachers. At the **[-1] full-last
layer** the PCA-aligned cosine is the less-stable metric for the highest-dim teachers (NT 2560, DNABERT-2
768 go negative), but the student still beats baseline for Enformer/Caduceus and silhouette/logit-r are
unchanged — i.e. feature preservation is robust to the layer choice; the [-2] cosine is the headline.

**NT-2.5B (2026-06-30, feature-aligned swap):** FIRST the all-NaN bug — the cached NT-2.5B base shard
`pytorch_model-00001-of-00002.bin` was **corrupt** (`zipfile.testzip` → bad member `data/313`, CRC fail),
silently loading layer-19 `intermediate.dense.weight` as NaN so every forward (fp32/bf16/merged/unmerged)
NaN'd from layer 19 on. Fix = quarantine + re-download the shard (verified clean); teacher NaN-free.
THEN the student swap: the original NT panel used the best-HP student which is `weight_mse=0` (NO feature
alignment) so its low/negative teacher-cosine was expected and did NOT demonstrate OmegaGenome's
feature-alignment. Swapped to a **feature-aligned original-size (0.12M/64-dim) NT splice student**
(`CE0.5 + MSE2`, kl=0, val 0.865; `OmegaGenome/outputs/nt_distillation/.../weight_mse2_.../student.pt`).
With feature alignment the **[-2] (paper-method) cosine flips POSITIVE and correctly orders** (student
**+0.048** > baseline −0.211, p=2e-100) — the feature-preservation claim now holds for NT. (The [-1] full-
last-layer cosine is more negative than baseline for this 2560-dim teacher; the [-2] panel — the
paper-faithful t-SNE feature — is the headline, and silhouette 0.43 vs 0.20 + logit-r 0.822 vs 0.676 both
favor the student at either layer.) Extractor saves BOTH layers; `feature_viz.py --layer {secondlast,last}`
renders either. NOTE: the earlier mse=0 NT panel was inserted into the docx this session and must be
REPLACED by these feature-aligned panels (docx edit deferred to the polish agent to avoid a write-race).

**Read (R1.13):** across every teacher, the distilled OmegaGenome student is MORE teacher-aligned than
the from-scratch BPNet (higher sample-wise cosine, all p≪0.001), and (DNABERT-2, Enformer) matches or
exceeds the teacher's own t-SNE silhouette. The absolute cosine magnitude scales with teacher↔student
representational compatibility and student quality (Enformer 0.40 ≫ DNABERT-2 0.08 ≳ Caduceus −0.05),
but the OmegaGenome-vs-baseline GAP is positive and significant for all teachers — feature preservation
is a teacher-agnostic property of the distillation, not an Enformer artefact. (Caduceus' student is a
weaker early-stopped run, val 0.7533, the only one saved on disk; its logit-r dips just below baseline
while its feature cosine/silhouette still clearly beat baseline.)

Caveats: the only NT splice student of the SAME onehot BPNet architecture available on `/extra` is an
`original`-size HP-search run (val 0.8976); the revision-fresh NT/DNABERT-2 students on the galaxy SSD
are *embedding-mode* (consume teacher embeddings, not sequence) and are not comparable in this
onehot teacher|student|baseline panel. No per-teacher DKD students exist for nt/dnabert2/caduceus (only
vanilla/OmegaGenome), so each per-teacher panel's "dkd" output duplicates its omega panel.
